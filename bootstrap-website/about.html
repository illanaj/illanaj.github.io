Bio

Born and raised in Virginia Beach, I've never minded the little bothersome things like sand in my car and water-logged ears. As I've gotten older and moved to the gorgeous Blue Ridge of Virginia Tech, I've carried that sentiment with me. Problems to me are essentially water off a duck's back, and I establish that in my work.

I didn't come into Virginia Tech as an Advertising major - I wasn't even a Professional and Technical Writing major. Instead, I came to Tech as a wide-eyed and bushy-tailed Creative Writing major. With all the confidence of a baby learning to walk, I stumbled through my first semester, weakly ending it with a B in Intro to Linguistics and a C- in Economics, a class I later learned I never actually needed.

It's all water off a duck's back, though, so I picked myself back up and switched to Professional and Technical Writing and, with enough reassurance, I added an Advertising major and later a Language Sciences minor. On this track, I've found myself blossoming in my classes, constantly learning new skills and developing new content with fervor. Once I started to get my hands on programs like Adobe and VS Code, I couldn't hold back from all the ideas fumbling around in my brain.
